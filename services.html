Paying for treatment

No surprise invoices, ever

We'd rather spend ten minutes on paperwork up front than have an awkward conversation at the front desk afterwards.

Insurance

We're in-network with most major carriers and file claims for you. Bring your card to the first visit and we'll verify benefits before treatment.

Treatment over $600 can be split across 6, 12 or 24 months at 0% interest, approved in the chair in about five minutes.

Questions

Things people ask us first

How soon can I get an appointment?
Checkups are usually available within five days, and we hold two slots each morning for emergencies. If you're in pain, call — we will find you a time today.
I'm genuinely scared of the dentist. What can you do?
Tell us when you book and we'll schedule a longer, treatment-free first visit: you stay dressed, sit up, and we just talk. From there we go at whatever pace you set, with a hand signal that stops everything instantly. Sedation is available for longer procedures.
Do you take my insurance?
We're in-network with most major plans and can submit out-of-network claims on your behalf. Send us your carrier and member ID through the contact form and we'll confirm your coverage before your visit.
Is the price I see the price I pay?
The figures on this page are self-pay minimums for straightforward cases. After your exam you get a written plan with the exact fee for your situation — that number is what you pay, and it doesn't change without your written approval.
Can the whole family come at once?
Yes. Ask the front desk for back-to-back family slots and we'll group everyone into a single trip. Saturday mornings are the easiest.
