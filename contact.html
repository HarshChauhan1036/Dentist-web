Before you come in

Useful to know

What should I bring to a first appointment?
Photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take. Arrive about ten minutes early to fill in a short medical history — or ask us to email the forms in advance.
How long will the first visit take?
Budget an hour. That covers a full exam, digital X-rays, gum charting and time to talk through what we found and what it would cost.
Can I change or cancel an appointment?
Of course — call or email at least 24 hours ahead and there's no charge. Short-notice cancellations may incur a $40 fee, which we waive for illness and emergencies.
Do you see patients who haven't been to a dentist in years?
Every week. Nobody here will lecture you about the gap. Say so when you book and we'll schedule extra time so it doesn't feel rushed.
